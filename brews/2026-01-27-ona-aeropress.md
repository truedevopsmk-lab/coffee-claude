## ⏱ Brew Steps (Inverted AeroPress)

1. Add **10 g** freshly ground coffee to inverted AeroPress  
2. Start timer and pour **40 ml** water  
   - Gentle swirl  
   - Bloom for **30 seconds**
3. Pour remaining water up to **160 ml** by **0:50**
4. Stir gently **2 times**
5. Steep until **2:00**
6. Flip AeroPress carefully
7. Press slowly from **2:05 → 2:35**
8. Stop pressing at first hiss

---

## 👅 Cup Profile & Notes

- **Aroma:** Soft caramel, mild fruit sweetness  
- **Taste:** Yellow fruit upfront, toffee-like mid, brown sugar finish  
- **Acidity:** Mild and rounded  
- **Body:** Medium  
- **Finish:** Clean and comforting  

Overall, a very easy-drinking cup with no sharp acidity. Works great as a relaxed morning or afternoon brew.

---

## 🔧 Adjustment Notes

- Increase temperature to **92°C** if the cup feels flat  
- Reduce water to **145 ml** for more body  
- Optional bypass of **15–20 ml hot water** for café-style balance  

---

## 📝 Journal Thoughts

A forgiving coffee and an excellent match for AeroPress. The washed Catimor shines with lower temperatures, highlighting sweetness over acidity. This recipe prioritizes comfort, clarity, and repeatability.
