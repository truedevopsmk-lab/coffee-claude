> 🗓 **Someday** — I don't own a cezve yet, and Turkish coffee needs a powder-fine grind that the C3S Pro can't safely reach (its finest 0–6 clicks are off-limits). Pre-ground Turkish coffee is the practical route.

{% include recipe-library.html method="cezve" %}

The **World Cezve/Ibrik Championship** sets no fixed dose or ratio. Its rule is a 50–110 ml traditional cup, filled to 5 mm below the rim including crema. 2016 champion Konstantinos Komninakis used 7 g to 60 ml, powder-fine, on a copper ibrik with flame control.
