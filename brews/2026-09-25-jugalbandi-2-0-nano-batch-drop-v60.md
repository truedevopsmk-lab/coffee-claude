## ⚖️ Brew Recipe (Winey Sweet)

* **Water:** 200 g
* **Ratio:** 1:16.7
* **Grind Size:** 16 clicks on Timemore Chestnut C3S Pro
* **Water Temperature:** 92°C
* **Brew Time:** ~2:45 target

---

## ⏱ Brew Steps (4:6 Sweetness-Focused)

1. Rinse the Daiso porcelain filter thoroughly with hot water and discard the rinse water.
2. Add 12 g of coffee and level the bed.
3. At **0:00**, pour **40 g** of water gently and evenly. Swirl once.
4. At **0:45**, pour **40 g**, bringing the total to **80 g**.
5. At **1:15**, pour **40 g**, bringing the total to **120 g**.
6. At **1:45**, pour **40 g**, bringing the total to **160 g**.
7. At **2:15**, pour **40 g**, bringing the total to **200 g**. Give the dripper one small swirl.
8. Allow the coffee to draw down completely, targeting approximately **2:45** total brew time.
9. Let the coffee cool for 2–3 minutes before evaluating the cup.
