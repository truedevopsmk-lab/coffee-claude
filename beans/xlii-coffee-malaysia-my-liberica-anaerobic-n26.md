### Extra Context

My Liberica Anaerobic N26 is a Malaysian Liberica coffee produced by My Liberica in Johor. The coffee is associated with anaerobic natural processing, with the N26 designation referring to a 26-day fermentation process described by the roaster. Liberica is a distinct coffee species from Arabica and Robusta, with a comparatively small share of global coffee production. The roaster's tasting profile highlights cherry, dark chocolate and Sarsi, a regional soft drink with a distinctive herbal, root-beer-like character.
