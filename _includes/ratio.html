{%- comment -%} Prints a brew ratio like "1:16.7" or "1:15" (no trailing .0). Params: dose, water. Coerces dose to a float first — Liquid's divided_by truncates on two integers. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _dose_f = include.dose | plus: 0.0 -%} {%- assign _ratio = include.water | divided_by: _dose_f | round: 1 | append: "" -%} {%- assign _tail = _ratio | slice: -2, 2 -%} {%- if _tail == ".0" -%}{%- assign _ratio = _ratio | split: "." | first -%}{%- endif -%} 1:{{ _ratio }}
