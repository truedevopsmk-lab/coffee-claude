### Flavor Notes (Roaster)

### Extra Context
Pepe Jijón is widely respected for refined, terroir-driven Ecuadorian coffees. Washed Mejorado lots are celebrated for elegance, sweetness, and clarity rather than intensity.

This coffee is especially well suited to pour-over brews highlighting nuance and balance.
