---

---

## ⏱ Brew Steps (Multi-Pour Structured)

1. Rinse filter thoroughly and preheat brewer and server.
2. Add 14 g coffee and level the bed.
3. Pour to 42 g for bloom. Swirl gently to ensure full saturation. Bloom for 35 seconds.
4. Second pour to 88 g total. Allow water level to draw down slightly.
5. Third pour to 130 g total.
6. Fourth pour to 172 g total.
7. Final pour to 210 g total and allow complete drawdown to finish around 2:50–3:00.

---

## 👅 Cup Profile & Notes

* **Aroma:** Soft florals with light citrus lift.
* **Taste:** Tea-like clarity with lemongrass brightness and mild toffee sweetness.
* **Acidity:** Gentle, citrus-leaning, not sharp.
* **Body:** Light to medium-light, structured like oolong tea.
* **Finish:** Clean initially; fades slightly as it cools.

The cup opened with delicate florals and a subtle citrus edge. As it cooled, the tea-like character became more pronounced, but sweetness plateaued, and the finish softened faster than expected.

---

## 🔧 Adjustment Notes

* Try slightly finer grind to improve sweetness development.
* Increase water temperature to 90–91°C for better extraction.
* Add slightly more agitation on second pour to enhance body.

---

## 📝 Journal Thoughts

This brew expressed the Ethiopian natural character in a restrained and elegant way. It leaned more toward tea structure than fruit-forward intensity, which made it pleasant but not explosive.

The sweetness was moderate and present, though I felt it could have been pushed further with minor extraction tweaks. The finish was clean while hot but tapered as it cooled.

Overall, a composed and balanced cup that rewards attentive brewing rather than aggressive extraction.
