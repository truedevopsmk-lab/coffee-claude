### Blue Tokai vs ARAMSE

| | Blue Tokai | ARAMSE |
|---|---|---|
| Ratio | 18 g : 100 ml (≈1:5.6) | 20 g : 120 g (1:6) |
| Temp | 95 °C | 94 °C |
| Bed | Levelled with the plunger, plunger left on | Stirred level, **no** tamper or umbrella |
| Drip time | 9:30–10:00 | 6–15 min |
| Milk | 50 ml decoction + 60 ml hot milk | Boiled milk, to taste |

Traditional kaapi often uses a medium-dark roast with up to 10–15 % chicory (ARAMSE).
