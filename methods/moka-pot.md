A Bialetti **Brikka** (2-cup) isn't a classic moka pot: its weighted valve holds pressure until ~1 bar and releases a burst with froth. Follow the Brikka's own rules (cold water, measured fill, lid closed) rather than classic moka-pot advice.

## ✍️ My notes

- Medium-fine grind
- Hot water in base
- Low heat extraction
- Focus on sweetness over bitterness

> ⚠ "Hot water in base" is classic moka-pot advice (Hoffmann). Bialetti's **Brikka** manual says **cold** water, filled with the measuring cup to 120 ml for the 2-cup. Worth trying both and noting which tastes better.
