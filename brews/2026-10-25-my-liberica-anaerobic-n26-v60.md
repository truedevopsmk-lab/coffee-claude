This entry documents my V60 4:6 recipe and brew notes for **My Liberica Anaerobic N26**, an anaerobic natural coffee from Johor, Malaysia.

---

## 📦 Coffee Details

* **Roaster:** XLIII Coffee
* **Coffee Name:** My Liberica Anaerobic N26
* **Origin:** Johor, Kulai, Simpang Renggam, Kampung Batu Nyamok, Malaysia
* **Estate / Farm:** My Liberica
* **Producer:** My Liberica
* **Process:** Anaerobic Natural
* **Varietal:** Liberica
* **Roast Type:** Extremely light

### Tasting Notes (from roaster)

* Cherry
* Dark chocolate
* Sarsi

---

## 🧰 Brewing Equipment

* **Brewer:** Daiso porcelain V60, 4:6 pour-over method
* **Grinder:** Timemore Chestnut C3S Pro
* **Scale:** InstaCuppa Rechargeable Coffee Scale
* **Server:** TIMEMORE Server 600ml Clear
* **Cup:** Borosil Thermo Cup (360 ml)

---

## ⚖️ Brew Recipe (Sweet and Syrupy)

* **Coffee:** 12 g
* **Water:** 180 g
* **Ratio:** 1:15
* **Grind Size:** 17 clicks on Timemore Chestnut C3S Pro
* **Water Temperature:** 94°C
* **Brew Time:** Not recorded

---

## ⏱ Brew Steps (V60 4:6 Method)

1. **0:00 — Bloom:** Pour 35 g of water evenly over the grounds.
2. **0:35 — First main pour:** Add 55 g, bringing the cumulative water to 90 g.
3. **Next pour:** Add 30 g, bringing the cumulative water to 120 g.
4. **Next pour:** Add 30 g, bringing the cumulative water to 150 g.
5. **Final pour:** Add 30 g, bringing the cumulative water to 180 g.
6. Allow the water to drain fully. Total brew time was not recorded.

---

## 👅 Cup Profile & Notes

* **Aroma:** Not recorded
* **Taste:** Lindt dark chocolate with cherries and plum
* **Acidity:** Bright and lively when hot
* **Body:** Syrupy as the coffee cooled
* **Finish:** Very sweet, like cherry jam and Lindt dark chocolate

The cup began with bright fruit character while hot, then developed a syrupy, jam-like sweetness as it cooled. The dark chocolate and cherry combination stayed prominent throughout.

---

## 🔧 Adjustment Notes

* Keep the water temperature at 94°C for the next brew.
* Retain the 17-click grind and 1:15 ratio as the baseline.
* Taste at multiple temperatures to track the shift in sweetness and body.

---

## 📝 Journal Thoughts

This was one of the yummiest coffees I have tried and easily one of my favourites.

When hot, it had a lovely brightness alongside dark chocolate, cherry and plum. As it cooled, the cup became syrupy and intensely sweet, reminding me of cherry jam with Lindt dark chocolate.

The changing cup profile made this brew especially enjoyable. I would happily return to this recipe as a baseline for the coffee.
